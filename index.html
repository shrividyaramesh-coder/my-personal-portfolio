ABOUT ME

I'm currently pursuing my PGP in Sports Management & Gaming at Masters' Union, after spending two and a half years as a Software Developer at Deloitte USI, working across cloud engineering, application migration, and stakeholder management.

Basketball has shaped most of my life. I've represented Tamil Nadu at the national level, captained my university team to multiple championship titles, and I'm still playing today. That competitive streak is what pulled me toward sports marketing.

Through internships with Freedom Global Sports Network, Dream Threads, and the Telangana Junior & Pro Basketball League, I've built content strategy, run brand campaigns for basketball jerseys, and managed on-ground operations from player auctions to championship day. I like being close to both the strategy and the execution.
